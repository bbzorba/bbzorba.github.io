Fenerbahçe Beko

Fenerbahçe’s basketball division has become a continental powerhouse, especially under coach Željko Obradović and Šarūnas Jasikevičius. Key Achievements: EuroLeague Champions (2016–17) – First and only Turkish team to win the EuroLeague. 5-time EuroLeague Final Four participants (2015–2019) – Demonstrated consistent elite performance. 10 Turkish League Titles (as of 2024) 7 Turkish Cups 7 Turkish Super Cups Notable Records: Most wins by a Turkish team in a single EuroLeague season. Undefeated Turkish League season (2017).

Fenerbahçe Beko Best Five 🏀

  • Bogdan Bogdanović – Serbian star, EuroLeague Final Four MVP, known for his clutch shooting and versatility.
  • Jan Veselý – Athletic Czech forward, EuroLeague MVP, famous for his dunks and energy.
  • Kostas Sloukas – Greek playmaker, key to Fenerbahçe’s EuroLeague title with his leadership and game management.
  • Ekpe Udoh – Defensive anchor, EuroLeague Final Four MVP, set records for blocks in the EuroLeague.
  • Melih Mahmutoğlu – Long-time captain, known for his three-point shooting and loyalty to the club.
