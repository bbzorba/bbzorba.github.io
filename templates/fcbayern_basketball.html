FC Bayern Munich Basketball

Although overshadowed historically by football, Bayern's basketball team has risen to prominence in recent years. Key Achievements: 5 German Championships (latest in 2023) 3 German Cups Regular EuroLeague participant since 2018 EuroLeague Playoff Appearances (2021, 2022) – Marking Bayern's growth in continental basketball. Notable Records: First German team to reach back-to-back EuroLeague playoffs. Highest win total in a single EuroLeague season for a German team.

Bayern Munich Basketball Best Five 🏀

  • Nihad Đedović – Bosnian guard, long-time leader, known for his scoring and consistency.
  • Vladimir Lučić – Serbian forward, captain, praised for his defense and clutch performances in EuroLeague.
  • Paul Zipser – German international, NBA experience, key for his versatility and athleticism.
  • Danilo Barthel – Former captain, instrumental in domestic titles and EuroLeague runs.
  • Petteri Koponen – Finnish sharpshooter, known for his three-point accuracy and experience.
