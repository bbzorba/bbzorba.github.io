Fenerbahçe

Fenerbahçe, founded in 1907, is one of the most successful and storied clubs in Turkish football history. Key Achievements: 28 Turkish Championships (19 Süper Lig titles, 6 National Division titles, 3 Turkish Football Championship titles) 6 Turkish Cups 9 Turkish Super Cups First Turkish team to win a UEFA group stage (2006–07 UEFA Cup) UEFA Champions League Quarter-Finalists (2007–08) – The club's best performance in Europe's elite competition. UEFA Europa League Semi-Finalists (2012–13) – A historic European run, narrowly missing the final. Notable Records: Longest unbeaten run at home in Süper Lig history. Highest points total in a Süper Lig season (93 points in 1988–89). Record Süper Lig win: 8–1 vs Gaziantepspor (1992).

Top Fenerbahçe XI ⚽

  • Alex de Souza – Legendary captain, Fenerbahçe's all-time top assist provider and a fan icon for his vision and leadership.
  • Lefter Küçükandonyadis – "Ordinaryüs," one of Turkey’s greatest ever, known for his sportsmanship and technical brilliance.
  • Roberto Carlos – The Brazilian left-back brought global attention to Fenerbahçe with his powerful free-kicks.
  • Volkan Demirel – Long-serving goalkeeper, famous for his passionate performances and leadership.
  • Emre Belözoğlu – Midfield maestro, known for his passing and tactical intelligence.
