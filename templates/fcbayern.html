FC Bayern Munich

Founded in 1900, Bayern Munich is synonymous with dominance in German and European football. Key Achievements: 33 Bundesliga Titles – The most in Germany. 20 DFB-Pokal Cups – German record. 6 UEFA Champions League Titles – Among Europe’s most elite (most recently in 2020). 2 Trebles (2012–13 and 2019–20) – Only club to win the continental treble twice. FIFA Club World Cup Winners (2013, 2020) Notable Records: Longest unbeaten streak in Bundesliga (53 matches). Most points in a Bundesliga season (91 in 2012–13). Most goals in a Bundesliga season (101 in 1971–72).

Top Bayern Munich XI ⚽

  • Philipp Lahm – Legendary captain, known for his intelligence, versatility, and leadership on and off the pitch.
  • Thomas Müller – "Raumdeuter," famous for his unique playing style, record assists, and sense of humor.
  • Robert Lewandowski – One of the world’s best strikers, broke Gerd Müller's Bundesliga goal record in a single season.
  • Franck Ribéry – Dazzling winger, part of the iconic "Robbery" duo, known for his dribbling and flair.
  • Manuel Neuer – Revolutionized the goalkeeper role as a "sweeper-keeper," multiple-time World’s Best Goalkeeper.
